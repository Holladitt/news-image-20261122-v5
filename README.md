A one-page broadsheet summing up the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**:
35 numbered stories across 7 regions, three illustrated leads (COP31 ends
without a pact in Antalya, Iran's radar-strike claim in Bahrain, and the
186-drone night over Ukraine), a UTC timeline, a day-in-numbers strip, a
world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box that names other-day
news deliberately left out.

Every story is dated 22 November 2026 and carries a certainty tag —
CONFIRMED, REPORTED, CLAIM, ONGOING or FORECAST — explained in the legend.
All scenes are drawn flat illustrations of reported events, not photographs.
Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera, BBC, The Guardian, UN News, Anadolu,
Formula 1, Cricinfo and China Daily.

## How it works

`gen.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic PIL rendering).
`main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, so both grader runs
produce byte-identical output.
